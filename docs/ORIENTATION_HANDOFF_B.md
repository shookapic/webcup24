# Orientation and plain language (D10, F89, F90, F91, F92): handoff from B to A

Status: **committed** on B's branch `world`; proven on a disposable scratch copy of A's committed `470aa42` (A's working tree untouched); **NOT integrated** in A's tree, **NOT deployed**. Contract: `coordination/reports/B-orientation-contract.md` (unchanged by the implementation).

## What it is
A small deterministic helper over the city's REAL data: the resident describes a need in their own words (FR or EN, accents/typos tolerated) and gets ranked services, places and portal procedures with a short reason, clarifying choices when the request is ambiguous, and a button that leads into the real portal (anchor + focus). On demand, a reviewed plain-language explanation appears under the untouched official text. It is NOT a chatbot: no AI service, no learning, no remote asset, no medical or legal advice. Matching runs in the browser; the resident's text is never sent, logged or stored.

## Data it reads (public columns only, whitelisted by a test)
`services` (title, description, details, EN fields, featured, availability, reason, return date, alternative) and `places` (kind, names, district, stop, address, hours, `open_24h`, `phone`). Anything staff add later is matched on its own text. Availability follows A's rule through `cityNow`.

## Behaviour that matters
- **Emergency first.** Wording such as "inconscient", "il respire plus", "crise cardiaque", "incendie", "heart attack", "I can't breathe" (about 100 FR/EN phrases, one typo tolerated for longer words) puts an emergency card above everything: "Appelez tout de suite le {numéro}", "n'écrivez pas un message à la ville", "je ne peux pas évaluer un état de santé", and the real emergency/hospital places with hours and a call link. The number is NEVER typed in the module: it is `places.phone` of emergency/hospital places, else the portal strip's own `tel:` link, else a pointer to the banner. Ordinary results sit behind a collapsed "Ce n'est pas une urgence vitale ?". Known limit: the bare word "feu" (also "fire") raises the card, a deliberate safe-side choice.
- **Ambiguous or weak** requests show "Vous parliez peut-être de…" choices; **no match** says so, offers common themes and the human fallback (write to the city, sign-in first if needed). Services the city does not list (passeport, permis de construire, carte de résident) are not invented.
- **Outage.** A service marked unavailable stays listed with reason, return date and alternative.
- **Plain language (F89/F90).** Reviewed explanations live in `PLAIN` in `orientation.mjs`, keyed by service TITLE, each with `facts` (names, conditions, steps) that a test verifies occur in BOTH the official text and the explanation (FR and EN); the button "Expliquer simplement" is on demand, the official text is always visible next to it, and a service without a reviewed explanation (or a staff-edited one with a new title) shows "Pas d'explication simplifiée relue pour ce texte : le voici tel quel". The explanations restate only what the seed texts say and were reviewed by B, not by the city: **the city should re-confirm them when a service text changes** (edit `PLAIN`; the facts test fails loudly if the original no longer contains a fact). A short "essentials in plain language" block covers the main procedures.
- **Privacy and bounds.** No query leaves the page (test: no request carries the typed text; storage and cookies unchanged), input capped at 300 characters / 40 tokens, index capped (5000+5000 entries rank in under a second), debounce 150 ms, stale ticket guard, nothing renders after `unmount()`, every string via `textContent`, destinations are fixed anchors (no staff URL).
- **Accessibility.** Labelled `type=search` field, polite status with count and first result, results in cards with headings, real buttons, focus stays in the field while typing, destination buttons move focus to the target, 320 px and 200 % zoom without horizontal scroll, text plus symbol for every state, FR/EN.
